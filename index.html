YouTube Uploader

YouTube Uploader is a private, single-owner tool. The channel owner runs it on their own computer to upload videos they made to YouTube channels they own, through the official YouTube Data API.

It is not offered to the public, and it does not access anyone else's account.

這是頻道擁有者自用的影片上傳工具，只會把擁有者自己製作的影片，透過 YouTube 官方 API 上傳到擁有者自己的頻道。這個工具不對外提供，也不會存取任何其他人的帳號。
